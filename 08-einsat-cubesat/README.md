# EinSAT — 3U CubeSat Space Telescope

**Project EinSAT, BITS Pilani** · Core member · Nov 2022 – Jun 2023

Prototyped the structure of a 3U CubeSat space telescope in Fusion 360. The mission concept is to detect transiting exoplanets from low Earth orbit. I researched CubeSat structural loads and common materials (Al 6061-T6, SS 316) to guide the layout.

## Files

- `cad/einsat-3u-telescope.step` — structure (STEP, opens in any CAD tool)
- `cad/einsat-3u-telescope-v3.f3d` — Fusion 360 source
- `cad/einsat-assignment.step` — earlier assignment model

**Tools:** Fusion 360 · **Skills:** space-structure concept design, research
